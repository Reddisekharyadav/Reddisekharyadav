## Featured real-world projects

These projects are kept at the top because they target concrete problems beyond a demo or tutorial.

### 1. [Allo Reservations Inventory](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/Allo-reservations-inventory)
**Problem:** inventory reservations can oversell stock when multiple users act at the same time.
**Solution:** a reservation-first, multi-warehouse system with atomic holds, row-level locking, and automatic release of expired reservations.
**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, cron jobs.
**Why it matters:** demonstrates concurrency-safe backend design for commerce and operations.

### 2. [Rural Healthcare Multilingual Chatbot](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/Rural-Healthcare-Multilingual-Chatbot-Platform)
**Problem:** rural communities can face language, access, and information barriers when seeking healthcare.
**Solution:** a multilingual assistant with health analytics, disease prediction, and government API integration.
**Stack:** Python, NLP/LLMs, machine learning, analytics, API integrations.
**Why it matters:** applies AI to accessibility and public-health communication.

### 3. [AI-Powered Smart Glasses](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/AI-powered-smart-glasses)
**Problem:** people with visual or situational challenges need hands-free context about their surroundings.
**Solution:** a companion app that captures images, accepts voice commands, and describes scenes through spoken audio.
**Stack:** React Native, TypeScript, BLE, Python, FastAPI, computer vision, text-to-speech.
**Why it matters:** connects mobile, hardware, and AI into an assistive experience.

### 4. [Meeseva Services Full-Stack Application](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/meesevaservices-Fullstack-Application)
**Problem:** local service businesses need a reliable way to manage branches, customers, and service delivery.
**Solution:** a production-oriented multi-branch service platform with cloud storage and secure APIs.
**Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Azure SQL Database, Azure Blob Storage.
**Why it matters:** models a real business workflow with cloud-ready infrastructure.

### 5. [Smart Parking Management System](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/Smart-Parking-Management-System-fullstack-application)
**Problem:** drivers waste time searching for available parking and operators lack real-time visibility.
**Solution:** location-aware parking management with availability, booking, payments, and live features.
**Stack:** MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js, maps/location services, payments.
**Why it matters:** turns an everyday urban problem into an end-to-end product.

### 6. [Fire and LPG Gas Detection](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/Fire-and-gas-detection-in-home-automation)
**Problem:** delayed awareness of fire or gas leaks increases household risk.
**Solution:** sensor-based detection with video evidence and Telegram alerts delivered in real time.
**Stack:** C++, embedded systems, sensors, video capture, Telegram integration.
**Why it matters:** prioritizes safety, fast notification, and actionable evidence.

### 7. [Eco-Loop Smart Building Control](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/Eco-Loop-Building-Agents-Autonomous-Smart-Building-Control)
**Problem:** buildings consume energy inefficiently when systems cannot coordinate decisions.
**Solution:** autonomous agents for smarter building control and energy-aware operation.
**Stack:** Python, autonomous agents, optimization, IoT/building data.

### 8. [LifeVault AI](https://github.com/Reddisekharyadav/LifeVault-AI-Your-Life.-Your-Memories.-Forever-)
**Problem:** memories are scattered across journals, photos, videos, and voice notes.
**Solution:** a privacy-first personal memory system with search, mood tracking, timelines, and AI insights.
**Stack:** Kotlin, AI, multimodal data, mobile application development.
